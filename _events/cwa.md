Our 2027 Church Weekend Away is taking place in Woodend from the 12th-14th of March. 
<!--excerpt end-->

Once a year, we head away together as a church for a weekend. It’s a chance to slow down, to eat, talk and laugh together, and to open God’s Word as a church family.

This year’s theme: How God changes people

All of us have a responsibility, and a privilege, to speak the gospel into each other’s lives. It’s not just the work of pastors or experts. As we speak to one another, and pray for one another, the Lord works through us to change us. This weekend we want to get better at that, and to be equipped for it.

Together we’ll look at the Psalms and what they show us about responding to trouble and suffering, both in our own lives and in how we help each other. Our hope is that we’d leave more confident that God is really at work changing people, and with a clearer picture of how each of us can help someone else follow Jesus.

Our speaker: Karl Hood

Karl has been a pastor and elder in the same church in Melbourne for over twenty years. He teaches pastoral counselling at PTC in Melbourne and is the executive director of Biblical Counselling Australia. He has also worked for many years as a GP in mental health, and he is currently doing a DPhil at Oxford on the Psalms and trauma. Karl is married to Louise, and they have four grown children.

As well as giving our talks, it’ll be a great chance to bring our questions and spend time with Karl and Louise over the weekend.

If you're not a happy camper, don't be put off. Despite the name, [Woodend Christian Camp](https://www.woodendcamp.co.nz/) has indoor twin and family rooms. There is a range of facilities to help [keep the kids busy](https://www.woodendcamp.co.nz/activities), and the site is a reasonable walk from the beach.
